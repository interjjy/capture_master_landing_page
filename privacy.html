Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 19, 2026

Jinyoub Jung (정진엽) [Capture Master] ("we", "us" or the "Operator") operates Capture Master, a macOS application (the "Software"), and its website. This Privacy Policy explains what personal information we process and how. We comply with the Personal Information Protection Act of the Republic of Korea and, where applicable, the EU and UK General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

At a Glance

  • Your screenshots, recordings, OCR text and Notion connection stay on your Mac. They are never sent to us.
  • OCR runs on your device, and captures are sent directly from your Mac to Notion.
  • We only process the minimum information needed for purchases, license verification and support.
  • We do not sell your personal information or use it for advertising.

  • Your captures: screenshots, screen recordings (MP4 and GIF) and OCR text are stored only on your Mac.

  • Usage data: we do not collect analytics, crash reports or advertising identifiers.

  • When you email us, we receive your message.

6. Deletion

  • We delete personal information without delay once the retention period ends or the purpose has been achieved.
  • Electronic files are deleted so that they cannot be recovered; paper records are shredded or incinerated.
  • Information we must keep by law is stored separately from other information.

7. Your Rights

  • You may at any time ask to access, correct or delete your personal information, ask us to restrict or stop processing it, object to processing, request a copy in a portable format, or withdraw your consent.
  • To exercise these rights, email interjjy@gmail.com. We will verify your identity and respond within 10 days.
  • We may be unable to delete information we are required to keep by law; if so, we will tell you why.
  • A legal representative or a person you authorize may exercise these rights on your behalf.
  • If you are in the EU or UK, you also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

8. Security

  • We process only the minimum information needed, and the Software is designed never to send us your captures.
  • Access to personal information is limited to the Operator alone.
  • Our Polar and email accounts are protected with two-factor authentication, and all connections use HTTPS encryption.
  • Your Notion access token is stored encrypted in the macOS Keychain.

9. Cookies

  • We do not use cookies or analytics tools on our website.
  • To display the website, your browser loads fonts and style files from third-party CDNs (Tailwind CSS, Google Fonts and jsDelivr). These providers may receive your IP address and other connection details.
  • When you open the checkout, Polar may use cookies necessary to process your payment, subject to Polar's Privacy Policy.

10. Children

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 14 (or under 16 where required by local law). If we learn that we have, we will delete it without delay.
